# Ullalu Day Card — Frozen Design

Status: Frozen for current product direction

## Collapsed Day Card

The collapsed card uses a calendar mental model and answers: **Where am I in my day?**

- Day/date, route/title, short subtitle, and free-time summary.
- Mini full-day time ribbon with semantic segment colors.
- Current-time marker when viewing an active day.
- Horizontally scrollable segment cards.
- Current/focused segment is centered initially.
- Previous and next segments remain partially visible to communicate continuity.
- Current segment is highlighted without changing its time-derived geometry.
- Users swipe/scroll left and right directly; no separate explanatory scroll-control row.

### Bottom row

One compact bottom row is used for either a note or a context-aware notification and also exposes the expand control.

Two message types are supported:

1. **User note** — entered, edited, and removed by the traveller.
2. **Context-aware notification** — generated by Ullalu from deterministic itinerary context such as time, route, opening hours, buffers, reservations, or conflicts. It does not require AI.

Examples:

- `📝 Buy Suica card at the airport.        ▼`
- `⚠ Only 35 min between arrival and dinner.  2 · ▼`
- If no message exists: `Add a note for this day        ▼`

If both a user note and a context notification exist, the most actionable context notification gets priority in the collapsed row; the count indicates additional messages.

## Expanded Day Card

The expanded card answers: **Show me everything about this day.**

- Expansion is vertical only; card width does not change.
- Full day summary strip at top.
- Chronological vertical itinerary timeline.
- Timeline items include time, type/icon, title, duration/context, and supporting details.
- Supporting context follows the timeline on mobile and can include route/map, travel totals, flight/train details, hotel, reservations, and notes.
- On larger layouts these details may be presented alongside the timeline only when doing so does not change the card's established width.

## Product rule

Collapsed = temporal orientation.
Expanded = itinerary inspection.

Both views render from the same underlying Day + Segment data model.

## Notes and AI

Context-aware notifications are part of the deterministic itinerary engine and must work with AI disabled. AI may later assist as a paid optional layer, but it does not own or replace Day Card logic.
